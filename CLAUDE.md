## Sources (details, terms and checks: `playbook/12-sources.md`)
- **MDARD "Michigan Restaurants and Commissaries"** (gisagomdard.state.mi.us FeatureServer): 34,310 active food service licenses from all
  45 local health departments, with points. Terms: no restrictions on reuse. The spine for "official" statewide. OrganizationName is the
  license holder: never shown.
- **Detroit Health Department inspections** (data.detroitmi.gov, the portal's own CSV download): shown as the City publishes them, the
  latest **routine** inspection's `is_in_compliance` ("In compliance" / "Not in compliance") plus Priority / Priority Foundation / Core
  counts. Attempted visits, closures and complaints all read "True" in that field, so they are never shown. No placard colour is implied.
  Detroit's portal publishes no license text; Nick approved showing the results as published in the public app (2026-10-22).
- **Detroit liquor licenses** (the City's monthly copy of the MLCC list): "serves alcohol" in Detroit only. The MLCC statewide list is on
  michigan.gov (bot check): Nick can download it by hand into `data/raw/official/manual/`.
- No other health department publishes in bulk (Oakland, Washtenaw, Wayne: search only; Kent, Kalamazoo: bot check; Ingham, Macomb:
  robots block). Ottawa County's ArcGIS layer has per-place report links (not wired in).
- Calibration (statewide vs MDARD): Meta + Google-open 77%, Meta ≥0.95 76%, Foursquare-only 6.6%.
- Press hosts: Metro Times answers our fetcher 403 (treat as blocked); Free Press, MLive, Detroit News, Eater, Record-Eagle, Lansing State
  Journal, Outlier, Crain's block. Usable: Visit Detroit, Deadline Detroit, WXYZ, Fox 2, Hour Detroit, uptravel.com, jamesbeard.org.
  **itunes.apple.com robots-blocks Claude**: don't use the iTunes Search API.

## Hand-checked guides
`pipeline/build_research.py` writes `data/research/app/*.json` and `jbf.json` from compact lists: each entry cites its 2025-26 page, the
address comes from our own data for that name in that town, and an undated page needs an active MDARD license (or a 2025-26 Detroit
routine inspection) as the evidence the place operates. Leads it couldn't confirm: `data/research/app/LEADS.md` (fish fries and supper
clubs, Flint coneys and oldest places not started). Guides with no places are hidden on Home and in the leaderboard.
