Michigan pączki

Fat Tuesday in Hamtramck

A pączek is a Polish doughnut of rich, eggy yeast dough fried and filled with fruit, custard or cream, then glazed or dusted with sugar. Metro Detroit eats them by the dozen on Pączki Day, the Tuesday before Lent, and Hamtramck's bakeries open in the small hours for the line. Most bakeries here make them only in the weeks before Lent.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Detroit (2), Livonia (2), Hamtramck (2), Ferndale (1), Pontiac (1), Berkley (1), Taylor (1), Rochester (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a 2025 or 2026 source backed it up (a dated local news story, an official tourism listing or the place's own site) and its street address matched our records. Where the source page carries no date, the place also had to hold an active food-service license on the state's list, or a 2025–26 Detroit inspection. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.
